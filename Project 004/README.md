# Client Project 004 – Customer Complaints & Support Performance Analysis

## Business Case Study

### Project Overview

This project analyses customer complaint records and support team performance to identify operational risks, customer experience issues, and opportunities for service improvement.

The analysis follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data quality assessment and cleaning through exploratory analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and management recommendations.

### Analytical Process

- Data Quality Assessment
- Data Cleaning & Validation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- KPI Development
- Executive Dashboard
- Management Report & Recommendations

### Key Areas Analysed

- Customer complaint patterns
- Complaint priority and resolution status
- Resolution time
- Customer satisfaction
- Complaint categories
- Support team performance
- High-priority operational risks

### Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- Filtering & Sorting
- Formulas
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Dashboard Design

### Key Business Objective

To provide management with data-driven insights that can support improved complaint resolution, customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and support team performance.

### Portfolio Note

This case study demonstrates an end-to-end practical data analytics workflow using a realistic business scenario, with emphasis on data quality, analytical thinking, business interpretation, and management decision support.
