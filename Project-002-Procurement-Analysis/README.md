# Project 002 – Procurement Performance Dashboard & Supplier Spend Analysis

## Business Scenario

A fictional organisation requested a comprehensive procurement analysis to evaluate supplier performance, purchasing patterns, procurement efficiency, purchase order status, lead times, and supplier quality. The objective was to identify opportunities for process improvement and support management decision-making through data-driven insights.

---

## Project Objectives

- Assess procurement data quality
- Clean and validate procurement records
- Analyse supplier spending
- Evaluate departmental purchasing
- Assess purchase order performance
- Analyse supplier delivery and quality
- Build an executive dashboard
- Provide practical business recommendations

---

## Business Value Delivered

This analysis provides management with visibility into procurement spending, supplier performance, purchase order efficiency, delivery reliability, and quality trends, enabling more informed operational and strategic procurement decisions.
